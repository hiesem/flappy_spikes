Flappy Spikes Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Thanks for playing Flappy Spikes. This page explains, in plain language, what information the game collects, why it collects it, and what it never does. If anything here is unclear, write to me at hello@duoleads.com and I will do my best to answer.

The short version

Flappy Spikes is a small arcade game. It keeps your best score on your device, sends anonymous gameplay statistics to Google Firebase Analytics, and, if you choose to sign in, shows your best score on the public leaderboards run by Google Play Games or Apple Game Center. It can also play short duels against other players' recorded runs — that is the only thing stored on my server, and it is anonymous. Ads are optional: a rewarded ad plays only if you tap to watch one. There are no accounts with me, and nothing is sold or shared with anyone else.

What the game collects

On your device. Your best score, the number of games you have played, and your sound setting are stored locally on your phone. This data never leaves your device.

Gameplay statistics. The game uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the game is played: when a game starts, when it ends, the score you reached, and whether you set a new best. Firebase also automatically records technical details like your device model, operating system version, app version, and an approximate country or city derived from your IP address. None of this tells me who you are. I use it only to see how many people play and where the game can be improved.

Leaderboards. If you sign in to Google Play Games (on Android) or Game Center (on iPhone), the game submits your best score to the public leaderboard under the nickname you chose for that service. Signing in is optional and handled entirely by Google or Apple. The game never sees your password and never gets access to your Google or Apple account beyond your player nickname and score.

Duels (versus). When you play a duel, the game sends an anonymous entry for that run to my server (Google Firebase, in the EU): a random player id, an automatically generated nickname, the items your bird is wearing, your score and level, and a replay of the run. It reads other anonymous entries to find you an opponent. If you link Google Play Games, the entry uses your Play Games display name and avatar instead of the generated nickname. No email, phone number or password is involved, and you can delete all of it from Settings at any time (see below).

Reminders. If you allow notifications, the game schedules local reminders on your phone. They are created and kept entirely on the device — nothing is sent to a server.

What the game does not do

No ad tracking: the rewarded ads are optional and are requested without using your device advertising ID. No account registration with me. No names, email addresses, contacts, photos, or precise locations collected. No data sold, rented, or shared with third parties for their own purposes. The only notifications are the local reminders you can switch off.

Third party services

The game relies on these services, each with its own privacy policy:

Google Firebase (usage statistics and the duel server)
Google AdMob (optional rewarded ads)
Google Play Games (leaderboards on Android)
Apple Game Center (leaderboards on iPhone)

Keeping and deleting your data

Local data (best score, games played, sound setting) stays on your device until you delete the app. Analytics data is kept by Google for a limited time and is not linked to your identity. Duel data is deleted automatically about 60 days after your last run. Leaderboard scores stay on Google Play Games or Game Center until you remove them there.

You can delete the duel data immediately from Settings → Delete my data: that removes your entry, your upload counter and your anonymous account, and resets your analytics identifier — coins, skins, levels and other local progress stay on your phone. If you want your analytics data deleted, email me at hello@duoleads.com from the device you play on and I will take care of it. You can also reset the game's analytics identifier at any time by reinstalling the app.

Children

Flappy Spikes is a general audience game, but it is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has submitted information through the game, please contact me and I will remove it.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects your privacy, this page will be updated before the change goes live, and the date at the top will show when that happened.

Contact
