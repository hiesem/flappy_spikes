Flappy Spikes Vertical

One endless world where every change of scene is a camera move. The camera sinks from the treetops to the hills, climbs to the city and on to the clockwork in the sky, drops to the giant desk and under it into Candy Land, climbs a giant book to the paper mountains, dives into the sea, rises into the dream, climbs into space and comes back down into the nest. The first six moves each pass through a floor of their own, with a set piece from the scenes on either side. The far layers move only a fraction of the camera's distance, so they still feel far away. Each stage lasts 40 hoops, four colour stages in the game.
