What the background does

The same rules for every ride. The highlighted step is playing.

    Game timings shared by all rides: the path at up to 480 pt/s, the launch flight 1.65 s, the glide 2.5 s, the settle 0.6 s; control returns with a flap and the first hoop reaches the bird 2 s later. The exits differ per ride (cannon 1.2 s, space 1.6 s, volcano 0.6 s, sea 1.3 s, pinball 2.0 s, storm 0.4 s). Sounds are listed under the ride name instead of played. Random layouts (stars, rocks, fish, rain) use their own seeded generator, so they sit in different places than in the game.
