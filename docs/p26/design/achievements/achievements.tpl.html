ACHIEVEMENTS

All 48 achievement icons, 512×512, in the 17C app-icon style: navy spike ring, glowing disc with white rays, offset drop shadow. Ranks carry the in-game badge; every other icon is a scene with a bird from the skin shop (body shapes, colours, patterns) wearing gear and hats from the shop, redrawn in the new big-eyed style.

NOTES

  • Collection: every icon shares the spike ring, the rays and the drop shadow. Most discs are the app icon's cyan; rides and a few others (Night Owl, Golden Egg, Rules the Roost, Hot Wings, Early Bird) get a themed disc for variety.
  • Birds: skin-shop birds redrawn in the 17C style: Classic, Sky, Tangerine, Grape, Lemonade, Coral dots, Camo, Snow, Mint, Robin, Chick, Penguin, Owl, Flamingo, Puffer, Buzz, Zombie, 8-Bit, Frost, Magma, Golden, Rainbow, Galaxy-style, plus a pigeon, magpie, hoopoe and peacock for the bird-name achievements.
  • Gear and hats: shades, heart shades, monocle, bow tie, chain, bandana, magic carpet, sweatband, cap, beanie, party hat, bucket hat, top hat, cowboy, propeller, chef, viking, wizard, crown — and a few extras (astro helmet, snorkel, beret, mail cap, night cap).
  • Each achievement needs one icon: Play greys it out while locked, Game Center shows its own placeholder.
