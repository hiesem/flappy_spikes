WHAT IS THIS FOR

Icons for the 10 new achievements of 2.6 (PRD-2.6 §7.1 D15, decision O94). Every achievement needs one picture in Play Games and Game Center. Players see it in the native achievements screen (now opened from the ACHIEVEMENTS tab of our leaderboard) and on the small toast on the results page when they earn it. The names, rules, points and visible / hidden flags are already approved (J9, O39, O77, O80, O82, O83; texts ACH-49 … ACH-58); only the pictures are new here.

Style: drawn exactly like the 48 approved icons (the "17C spike-ring style"): the same navy spike ring, the glowing disc with white rays, the offset blue drop shadow, and the same big-eyed bird. The new art only adds scenes; the 48 icons do not change. In the versus icons you are always the pink bird with the gold sweatband and the rival the blue bird with the red band, as in the approved BEAK TO BEAK.

On this page: (1) each new icon large, with its rule and points, and for three of them a second idea B with a recommendation; (2) the icons at the real sizes and on the dark and light backgrounds the consoles use; (3) the new icons mixed in with approved ones, and the whole set of 58. What you approve: the 10 pictures (choose A or B where there is a B). After your OK, Sol exports the PNGs in the build (512 for Play, 1024 for App Store Connect, 160 for the in-game toast), just like the 48.
