DAILY MISSIONS · 2.6

WHAT IS THIS FOR

What the player sees: three small goals a day (easy, medium, hard), for example "Pass 80 hoops". They pay 20 / 35 / 55 coins, paid automatically when the run that finishes them ends. Finishing all three opens a bonus chest: a golden feather, else a puzzle piece, else 40 coins.

Where: a slim MISSIONS strip under START on Home with three pips (tap it to open the panel). The panel lists the missions with progress bars, the countdown "NEW IN 5 H 12 M" and one optional swap by video a day. The WEEKLY COURSE card sits at the top of the panel once the course is unlocked. On the results page the pips fill and the coins fly into YOUR REWARD. The chest opens on the next Home visit, as that visit's one popup.

PANEL STATE

Swap: one mission a day can be swapped for another of the same slot by watching a rewarded video. "SWAP" shows on unfinished rows while the swap is unused and an ad can be shown (after consent; never offered when no ad is loaded). After a swap, every SWAP button is gone until tomorrow. There is no explanation line under the list (owner): the video icon on SWAP says it.

Colours: stripe colours only: the easy / medium / hard missions get a soft pink / pink / plum stripe on the left. The rows carry no EASY / MEDIUM / HARD label (round 23, O71); the stripes are all pink-family shades, so no new colour comes in.

3 · RESULTS PAGE: PIPS FILL, COINS FLY

The same strip sits under the reward row, so the results page gets no new element type. After the page has settled, each mission this run moved fills its pip, and the label briefly names the mission. A finished one pops its check and a "+20" chip, and the coins fly into YOUR REWARD, which counts up. The rewards are already saved at run end, so killing the app here loses nothing. The strip only shows when a mission moved in this run.
