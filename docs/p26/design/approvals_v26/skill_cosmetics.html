WHAT IS THIS FOR

Skill-only cosmetics (PRD 2.6 WP-F, F10 · approvals §7 D10 + names and unlock line T10). 24 items that cannot be bought, with coins or with money: 3 of each kind (hoops, trails, hats, birds, gear) and 9 on the versus card (3 frames, 3 name styles, 3 victory stickers). UPDATED · ROUND 23 +6 card items (owner, versus page). Each one is unlocked by one achievement (SkinItem.unlock: AchievementId), so wearing one shows what you have done. They are never drawn as level or Star Road rewards. UPDATED 10 new items; Steel Spikes and Legend Ring got more unique, more animated art.

What the player sees: in the shop, each tab ends with its 3 skill items under "EARNED BY SKILL · NOT SOLD". DECIDED They wear LEGENDARY gold (bar, chip, reveal box) with a small trophy mark. A locked tile shows its goal where the price would be; when the achievement is secret it shows "? ? ?" until earned. Tapping it tries the item on and shows "UNLOCK: SCORE 1,000 POINTS IN ONE RUN" with your progress. UPDATED · ROUND 25 Every skill item is secret in the shop (owner, Q32 approved, same rule as the card items, O85): a locked tile shows "? ? ?" where the price would be in every tab; tapping it tries the item on and shows "UNLOCK: ? A SECRET ACHIEVEMENT ?", with no goal and no progress bar. The achievements themselves stay as they are in Play Games / Game Center (visible ones stay visible there), so a player who wants to know can look there. Earned: "UNLOCKED BY: <achievement>". When the achievement is earned, the results page plays the approved item reveal (style A, the box) in LEGENDARY gold with a plum ribbon: ACHIEVEMENT REWARD → item → EQUIP / LATER, with the legendary extra ring and sound (lv_up_big).

DECIDED Two new achievements (option A): BULLSEYE 40 and SKY HIGH 50 (points approved as listed). UPDATED · ROUND 23 No special path for 2.5 players (there are none): no first-launch grant, no "returning player" state. A skill item is owned when its achievement is earned, that's all. Skill items count in the shop's "x / total" bar and the tab counters. A level item and a skill item from one run play as two reveals in a row.

How to use this page: SHOP shows any tab; tap the skill tiles. UNLOCK MOMENT replays the reveal for the item you pick (REPLAY plays the sound). All 24 items are drawn larger in the catalogue under the phone.
