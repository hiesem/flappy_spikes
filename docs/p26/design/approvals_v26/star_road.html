WHAT IS THIS FOR

Star Road is a free 28-day season (PRD-2.6 WP-G G6, progression report §3.8). Every daily mission the player finishes also drops stars: easy 1, medium 2, hard 3, the first finished run of the day 2 (the "bonus"), and +3 when all three missions are done: at most 11 a day. Every 8 stars reach the next of 25 tiers (200 stars in all; about 18 perfect days, or 20–22 days for a normal daily player). Seasons run back to back. DECIDED the word stays "stars", the bonus +2 stays, the coin tiers stay as they are.

Rewards are existing things only: 25–75 coins on 20 tiers, a golden feather on tier 5, a puzzle piece on tier 15, and a random item you don't own yet on tier 10 (RARE), 20 (EPIC) and 25 (LEGENDARY). Item tiers open with the approved mystery-box reveal (Level Item Reveal, style A). DECIDED If every item of that rarity is owned the tier pays 250 / 500 / 1 000 coins; a 4th feather or piece pays 50 coins. Tiers reached but not claimed are added by themselves when the season ends.

Where it lives: no new Home button and no new tab. It sits as a card at the bottom of the daily-missions panel (the weekly course keeps the top slot); on 568 pt phones the card folds into one line. The Levels screen's tabs (LEVELS · WORLDS · GUIDE · ALBUM) stay as they are. Stars are shown next to each mission's coin reward. UPDATED When a tier becomes claimable, the next Home visit shows one Star Road popup with a GO TO STAR ROAD button (inside the one-popup-per-visit budget, no NEW! sticker; section below the phone). No purchase, no paid track, no ad needed to earn stars or claim anything, so it is not an IAP or loot-box feature on Google Play or the App Store.

Pick a state on the left. On the road screen the CLAIM buttons work: coin tiers fly coins to the wallet, item tiers open the reveal (with sound: turn your volume on).

What: one P1 popup on Home when at least one Star Road tier can be claimed. It shows the tier badge on slowly turning rays, the waiting reward(s) as their road tiles (coins, feather, piece, or the closed mystery box in its rarity colour), and one button: GO TO STAR ROAD, which opens the road scrolled to the first CLAIM. The × closes it; the tiers simply wait on the road.

When: DECIDED · ROUND 23 on the first Home visit after a tier became claimable (stars are added when a mission finishes on the results page, so this is usually the Home visit right after that run), at most once a day. It is one of the C2 popups: LEVEL UP catch-up → streak reward → missions chest → Star Road → No-Ads → rating. If a popup higher in the list takes this visit, the Star Road popup waits for the next visit. The chest and the popup never come in one visit. Never in runs 1–3 (missions, and so stars, start at game 4). Not shown at season end: the summary stays where it was (missions panel).

Owner rules: no NEW! sticker, no unlock card, no badge on the tiles, no autoplay sound; nothing to buy and no ad. Sound in the game: sk_dialog when it pops (here a soft synth chime after you press REPLAY).

Pace. 8 stars a tier · 200 stars for tier 25. Perfect play: 11 a day = tier 25 on day 19. Normal daily player (easy + medium + bonus most days, all three about every other day): ~8–10 a day = tier 25 around day 20–25. A casual player (5 a day) ends near tier 17 and still gets the RARE item.

Coins. 960 coins over the season (≈ 34 a day for a player who finishes it) plus 2 consumables and 3 items (≈ 7 750 coins of shop value, per the report).

Season clock. Seasons are fixed 28-day blocks counted from a compiled-in start date (no server, no remote config), switching at the player's local midnight, the same moment missions refresh. Season 4 starts the moment season 3 ends: there is never a day without a road.

Who sees it. It unlocks together with daily missions (showcase done + 1 run, G1). Stars only come from missions, so showcase runs never meet it. Players who join mid-season start at tier 0 with the days that are left.
