BEAT YOUR BEST

2.6 WP-K, the in-run goal. In Free Fly a BEST chip sits in the free middle of the HUD row, and a gold marker scrolls in at the hoop where your best run ended. Passing it plays the NEW BEST sticker (the GoldSticker master, 1.2 s, under the HUD), and the chip turns gold and counts your live score.

Example: best 42 points, ended at hoop 43 (index 42). Points = hoops here; in a real run a streak can beat the best before the line. The moment fires on points, the line is only a "where".

The chip lives in the top row between the heart and the pause button, the spot the versus bar uses (the two never show together). Versus with no opponent (solo, offline, empty pool) looks the same as Free Fly.
