Delete your data

Your Flappy Spikes account and versus data · Last updated: October 13, 2026

Flappy Spikes creates an anonymous account for versus races (linked to Google Play Games if you sign in there). You can delete that account and its data at any time, with or without the app.

1. In the app

Open Settings → Delete my data and tap DELETE. This deletes, right away:

  • your versus entries (your recorded runs and your versus profile);
  • your profile picture copy, if you linked Google Play Games;
  • your account: the anonymous one, or the one linked to Google Play Games;
  • your upload counter;
  • and it resets your analytics identifier.

Your coins, skins, levels and other progress stay on your phone, so you can keep playing. To remove those too, delete the app.

2. Without the app

Email hello@duoleads.com with the subject "Delete my data". If you can, add your Google Play Games name or your versus nickname, so we can find your entry. We answer within 30 days.

You don't have to do anything for versus data you no longer use: it is deleted automatically about 60 days after your last run.

3. What is kept, and for how long

  • Analytics data is kept as long as Google Analytics retains it; it is no longer tied to you once your analytics identifier is reset.
  • Purchase records are kept by Google Play, as the law requires.
  • Leaderboard scores stay on Google Play Games until you remove them there.
  • Ad data is kept by Google and Unity under their own policies; you can reset your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

More about what the game collects is in the privacy policy. Questions about the game itself: the support page.
