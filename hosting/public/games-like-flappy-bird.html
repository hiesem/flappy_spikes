01The genre

What makes a one-tap flying game

A one-tap flying game has one control: tap and your character jumps or flaps, let go and gravity pulls it down. The whole skill is timing. Runs are short, a mistake ends them, and "one more go" is always a tap away. The best games in the genre are easy to learn, hard to master and fair: when you crash, you know it was your tap.

Flappy Spikes has been one of them since 2014: tap to fly, thread the bird through the hoops and stay clear of the spikes on the top and the bottom of the screen. The how to play guide covers the details.

The classic

If you like Flappy Bird

Flappy Bird, released in 2013, is the game that made one-tap flying famous: you guide a small bird through gaps between pipes.

In Flappy Spikes

Flappy Spikes started the same way, with pipes, in 2014. Today the pipes are hoops: you fly through rings instead of between pipes, a rim touch bounces you off instead of ending the run, and clean passes build a perfect streak that sets your bird on fire. The bird still flaps with one tap, and the spike strips on the top and the bottom are still deadly.

The wall bouncer

If you like Don't Touch the Spikes

Don't Touch the Spikes, by Ketchapp, has you bounce a bird between two walls while spikes appear along the sides.

In Flappy Spikes

Flappy Spikes keeps the spikes but sends you forward instead of side to side: the screen scrolls, the spike strips run along the top and the bottom, and the hoops come at you one after another. The colours change every 10 hoops, and the game gets faster the further you fly.

The hoop shooter

If you like Flappy Dunk

Flappy Dunk, by Voodoo, has you flap a winged basketball through hoops, with bonus points for clean shots.

In Flappy Spikes

In Flappy Spikes you thread a bird through hoops too, and a pass without touching the rim is a perfect. Perfects in a row score more and more points, and the third one lights the fire. On top of the hoops there are special rings: springs, switches, saws, ring trains and portals to rides.

The fantasy flyer

If you like Flappy Dragon

Flappy Dragon puts the one-tap flying idea in a fantasy setting, with a small dragon flapping past obstacles.

In Flappy Spikes

Flappy Spikes has its own flock instead of one hero: 30 birds to unlock, from a rubber duck to a phoenix, plus hats, gear, hoops and trails. See them all on the birds and skins page.
