3RULES
How to play

Three rules. That's it.

Flappy Spikes is a one-tap flying game with hoops and spikes. Tap to fly, keep your bird in the air and thread it through every hoop.

How to play, power-ups and tips
1
The bird flaps once per tap.Tap to fly

Every tap is one flap. Let go and gravity takes over.

2
The bird flies through a hoop for a perfect.Fly through the hoops

Fly through without touching the rim for a perfect. Three perfects in a row and your bird catches fire.

3
One touch of the spikes ends the run.Flap fast, fly far

Stay clear of the spikes, top or bottom: one touch ends the run. A shield or a heart saves you once.

Versus

Race real players.

Fly against a real player's run. Their score climbs live at the top of your screen as their recorded run plays back. Beat it to win.

Race real players every run, or switch to Free Fly, and chase the top of the Google Play Games leaderboard.

FAQ

Questions?

Is Flappy Spikes free?

Yes. Flappy Spikes is free to play, with ads. An optional No Ads purchase removes them.

Is Flappy Spikes on iPhone?

Yes. Flappy Spikes is free on the App Store for iPhone and iPad, and on Google Play for Android.

How do you play Flappy Spikes?

Three rules: tap to fly, fly through the hoops, and stay clear of the spikes. Read the full guide to playing Flappy Spikes.

What is Versus?

In Versus you race a real player's recorded run. Their score climbs at the top of your screen while you fly; beat it to win. More about Versus.

Is this the original Flappy Spikes from 2014?

Yes. It is the same game that first flew on iPhone in 2014, rebuilt from scratch in 2026. Read the Flappy Spikes story.
