Delete your data

Your Flappy Spikes account and versus data · Last updated: October 19, 2026

The quickest way is in the app. Open Settings → DELETE MY DATA. It happens right away. Without the app, see section 2.

Flappy Spikes, made by Duo Leads, keeps your versus data on our server: your recorded runs, your versus profile and the account they belong to. This page says what is deleted, what is kept, and how to ask us when you cannot use the app.

1. In the app

Open Settings → DELETE MY DATA and tap DELETE to confirm. This deletes, right away:

  • your versus entries: your recorded runs, with their scores and replays, that other players can race;
  • your versus profile: your generated nickname and your bird's look in versus (the items it wears);
  • your custom name, if you chose one, and any claim on that name, so another player can use it;
  • your account on our server, whether anonymous or linked to Google Play Games or Game Center. The link is removed. Your Play Games or Game Center account itself is not changed.

If the app cannot reach our server, nothing is deleted and the app tells you so. Try again when you are online.

On your phone, your coins, skins and the rewards you have claimed stay, so you can keep playing. Your level goes back to unranked (a level you reach again does not pay its reward twice), and your music volume goes back to the default. To remove everything on the phone, delete the app.

2. Without the app

Email hello@duoleads.com{{noscript_email_pink}} with the subject "Delete my data". Include the player name you used in versus (your custom name, or the generated one, like SwiftKiwi482), so we can find your data. We may ask you for proof that the account is yours before we delete anything, because a name on its own does not prove who you are. We answer within 30 days.

3. What is kept, and for how long

  • Google Play Games and Game Center keep your sign-in and the scores and achievements the game sent to them. Google and Apple hold that data, and you manage it in Play Games or Game Center.
  • Purchase records are kept by Google Play or the App Store, as the law requires.
  • Analytics data is kept as long as Google Analytics retains it. It is no longer tied to you once your analytics identifier is reset, which deleting in the app does.
  • Ad data is kept by Google and Unity under their own policies; you can reset your advertising ID in your phone's settings.
  • Versus data you stop using is deleted automatically about 60 days after your last run, so you do not need to do anything.
  • Custom names are released after 30 days of inactivity if you are not signed in to Google Play Games or Game Center, so another player can use them. Deleting your data releases yours at once.

More about what the game collects is in the privacy policy. Questions about the game itself: the support page.
