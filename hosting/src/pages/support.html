Support

Help with Flappy Spikes · Last updated: October 13, 2026

Something not working, or a question about the game? Most answers are below. If yours isn't, mail us: we read every message.

Mail us

Or copy the address: hello@duoleads.com{{noscript_email_pink}}. Tell us your phone model and what happened; for a purchase, add the order number from your Google Play receipt (it starts with GPA).

Purchases

I bought coins or NO ADS, but nothing arrived

A purchase arrives as soon as Google Play confirms it. If it hasn't, check that you're online and restart the game: it looks for unfinished purchases when it starts and when you come back to it. A payment that is still pending (for example cash or a bank transfer) arrives once Google confirms it. Still missing after a day? Mail us with the order number.

How do I get NO ADS back on a new phone?

Use the same Google account in the Play Store, then open Settings → Restore purchases. NO ADS comes back. Coins, skins and levels are saved on the phone itself, so they don't move to a new phone.

I have a gift code

Open Settings → Redeem code. It opens the Play Store's redeem screen; after you redeem the code, come back to the game and the gift arrives with a popup.

Ads

Why do I see ads?

Flappy Spikes is free, and ads pay for it: a banner during runs and a video ad after some runs. New players see no video ad until they have played 5 runs or 5 minutes. Rewarded ads only play when you choose them, for example for free coins in the Shop or a revive.

How do I remove the ads?

Buy NO ADS in the Shop. It removes the banner, the ads after runs and the NO ADS offer. Rewarded ads stay available if you want the coins, and NO ADS owners get the first revive of a run for free.

How do I change my ad consent?

In the EU, the UK and a few other regions the game asks for your consent before showing ads. To change your choice, open Settings → Privacy options (the row only shows where consent is required).

Google Play Games and the leaderboard

How do I sign in and see the leaderboard?

If you use Google Play Games, the game signs you in by itself. To sign in by hand or to open the leaderboard, tap the trophy next to the gear on the Home screen, or the leaderboard button on the results page. The Top Scores leaderboard shows your best score. Signing in is optional; the game works the same without it.

Versus

How does Versus work?

In Versus you race a real player's recorded run: their score climbs in the bar at the top of your screen while you fly, and the higher final score wins. A win pays 10 coins, a draw 5. Prefer to fly alone? Switch the mode button above START to FREE FLY. More on the Versus page.

Can I change my name in Versus?

Yes. In Settings you can choose a custom name of 3 to 14 letters and digits. A word filter checks it, and each name can be held by one player. Without one, the game makes up a nickname for you, like SwiftKiwi482. Signing in to Google Play Games or Game Center doesn't change either name: your platform name and picture are never used. Your picture in Versus is your own bird, in the hat and gear you picked.

Deleting your data

Open Settings → DELETE MY DATA, or see the delete your data page for what is deleted and how to do it without the app.

Sound and music

No sound or no music?

Open Settings: the SOUND and MUSIC tiles switch the effects and the music on or off, and the MUSIC VOLUME bars set how loud the music plays. The game also follows your phone's media volume, so turn that up too.

Other questions

Why are the Shop and Skins locked?

They open once you score 25 points in a run. Until then, just play: your coins are waiting for you.

Where is my privacy information?

Everything about the data the game uses is in the privacy policy.
