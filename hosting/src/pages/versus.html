01The race

How a race works

1
Tap START

Versus is the default mode on the Home screen. Tap START and the VS screen finds you a rival while it spins up: another player's nickname and their bird, in their look. Then your run starts as usual, and you wait for your first tap.

2
Race a recorded run

Your rival isn't flying live. You race a recorded run: when that player flew, the game saved when each of their points came in. During your run their score replays on the same clock, point by point, so it feels like they're flying right next to you.

3
No waiting

Recorded runs mean there is always someone to race, any time, with no waiting for a lobby and no lag.

02On screen

The versus bar

At the top of the screen, the versus bar shows the race: your score in the pink box on the left, theirs on the right, with VS in the middle and both names underneath. Their box flashes gold every time their score goes up, and dims once their recorded run has ended. Then it's just you against their final score.

03Your rival

Matching

The game first looks for a run from a player at your rank that scored less than your best, so the race is one you can win; if there is none, any recent run will do. Players you haven't met for a while come first, and you are never matched against your own runs. If no rival turns up within a few seconds, the VS screen says NO RIVAL FOUND and you fly solo; the next run tries again.

  1. A player at your rank who scored less than your best
  2. Any recent run, players you haven't met for a while first
  3. NO RIVAL FOUND: you fly solo, the next run tries again
04The results

Win, lose or draw

When your run ends, the results page compares the two final scores. A higher score wins and pays 10 coins; the same score is a draw and pays 5 coins. Lose and you still keep everything you earned in the run. Every run, won or lost, also counts toward your best score and your rank.

05Solo

Free Fly if you'd rather fly solo

Not in the mood for a race? The mode button above START switches between VERSUS and FREE FLY. Free Fly is the classic game: no rival, no VS screen, just you, the hoops and the spikes. The how to play guide covers everything that happens in a run.

06Who you are

Your nickname

RapidParrot411

Other players see you under a nickname the game makes up for you, like RapidParrot411: an adjective, a bird and three digits, so there is never any free text. Signing in to Google Play Games or Game Center doesn't change it: platform names and pictures are never used. Your picture is your own bird as a sticker, in the hat and gear you picked, so dress it up.

07Privacy

Your data

To be raced, some of your runs are stored on our server with an anonymous account: your nickname, your look, your score and the replay of the run. Versus data is deleted automatically about 60 days after your last run, and you can delete it any time from Settings, or by email. Everything is explained on the delete your data page and in the privacy policy.
