Is Flappy Spikes free?

Yes. Flappy Spikes is free to play, with ads. An optional No Ads purchase removes them.

Is Flappy Spikes on iPhone?

Yes. Flappy Spikes is free on the App Store for iPhone and iPad, and on Google Play for Android.

How do you play Flappy Spikes?

Three rules: tap to fly, fly through the hoops, and stay clear of the spikes. Read the full guide to playing Flappy Spikes.

What is Versus?

In Versus you race a real player's recorded run. Their score climbs at the top of your screen while you fly; beat it to win. More about Versus.

Is this the original Flappy Spikes from 2014?

Yes. It is the same game that first flew on iPhone in 2014, rebuilt from scratch in 2026. Read the Flappy Spikes story.
